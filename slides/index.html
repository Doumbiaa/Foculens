1 The idea

FocusLens notices when Alice stops, and pauses the timer.

Reads your face from the webcamHead turn, head tilt, eyes closed
Pauses the timer when you look awayAfter a short grace period, and says why
Runs in your browserNo video leaves your device
Not new in principle: seeTV paused a video when you looked away (Dickie et al., 2005). We do it for studying, on a laptop webcam.

2 Setup

From a webcam frame to a paused timer

Input

Webcam

Your laptop camera. Frames stay on the page.

Landmarks

MediaPipe Face Landmarker

About 20 ms per frame on a CPU. No GPU.

Features

13 numbers per frame

Head pose, blink scores, eye gaze.

yawpitchrollblink L/Rgaze
Decision

Detector

Rules, MLP, or both. Pauses after a 5 s grace, resumes 0.3 s after you are back.

Calibration first: 3 seconds sitting normally set Alice's own "straight ahead".

Models compared on our data: logistic regression, random forest, gradient boosting and an MLP. Score: macro F1, cross-validated with whole people held out.

4 The app

Three detectors, one switch

Rules only

Thresholds

Head turn or tilt past 15° from your sitting point.

Eyes closed: blink score 0.5 or more.

Presets: strict 15°, balanced 20°, relaxed 25°.

Explains itself. Needs no data.
MLP classifier

Learned

13 features in, focused or not out.

One hidden layer of 64 units, trained on our 12 participants.

Never sees the angle limits. Learns them.

Can learn what rules miss.
Both models

Hybrid

The MLP decides whether you are focused.

The rules give the reason: left, right, down, up, eyes closed.

A pause always comes with a why.

Verdict from one, reason from the other.

All three: 5 s grace, ignore anything under 0.5 s, resume after 0.3 s back in range.

5 Where it fails

Three situations it gets wrong

More than one face

What happens

Someone walks behind you or sits beside you, and the pauses go wrong.

Next: follow only the largest face.

Thinking

What happens

Looking up to think reads as a lapse: a false pause. Alice's 5 lost minutes, eyes on screen, read as focus: a miss.

Next: a thinking mode.

Taking notes

What happens

Head down to write looks like the phone. Notes mode allows it, but detection drops from 83% to 73%.

Next: tell paper from phone.

6 What it means

A camera sees posture. Attention is inside.

How models see

Proxies, not attention

The model sees head angle and eyelids. It works while posture and attention move together, and fails when they separate.

How people see

Context decides

A classmate tells thinking from scrolling by what is on the desk and how long it lasts. Our model has no context.

With another week

Close the gaps

Thinking mode. One-face tracking. Automatic notes detection. Live false pauses per minute. More people, more varied.
