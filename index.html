◎ FocusLens

WELCOME

Focus on your work. Let FocusLens watch the timer.

FocusLens is a focus timer that uses your webcam to estimate whether you are staying attentive. It observes your face position and eye state, then pauses your focus session when you look away and resumes when you return.

Your camera feed is processed on your device. FocusLens does not record or upload your video.

HOW IT WORKS

A combination of lightweight vision models

MediaPipe Face Landmarker detects facial landmarks from your webcam image.

FocusLens Rules evaluate head direction and eye state using configurable thresholds.

MLP classifier classifies each frame as Focused or Not focused. You can choose Rules only, MLP classifier, or Both models.
